Flood safety checklist

  • Move to higher ground if water is rising.
  • Stay away from flooded roads, drains and underpasses.
  • Never walk or drive through moving floodwater.
  • Keep your phone charged and follow local authority instructions.
  • Help children, older adults and people who may need assistance, if safe.

Report a local waterlogging concern

Send a report to this prototype's local admin console. It is not an emergency dispatch channel.

RESPONSE GUIDANCE

Preparedness information

QUICK REFERENCE
Before heavy rainfall

Check weather updates, identify safer routes, keep essential items accessible and avoid parking in low-lying areas.

During waterlogging

Avoid underpasses and flowing water. Do not touch fallen electrical wires or electrical equipment exposed to water.

After water recedes

Wait for local clearance before returning to affected areas. Avoid contact with contaminated standing water.

Safety information is general guidance, not a substitute for emergency responders or official local instructions.
